## Key Takeaways

- **Total combined revenue**: ~$4.16 trillion across the top 10.
- **Most profitable**: Alphabet ($100.1B net income) overtook Apple, becoming the first in its sector to surpass $100B annual profit.
- **Largest employer**: Walmart at ~2.1M employees — the world's largest private employer.
- **Healthcare dominance**: 4 of the top 10 (UnitedHealth, CVS, McKesson, Cencora) are in healthcare, reflecting the sector's massive scale.
- **Highest profit margin**: Apple (~24.0%) and Alphabet (~28.6%) lead on profitability despite lower revenue than Walmart/Amazon.
